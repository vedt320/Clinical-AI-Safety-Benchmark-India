# Clinical AI Safety Benchmark — India

## About
This project aims to evaluate the safety, accuracy, and reliability of AI-generated clinical answers, with a focus on India's diverse population.

## Objectives
- Identify medical errors in AI-generated answers.
- Evaluate whether AI recognises medical emergencies.
- Compare AI responses with trusted clinical guidelines.
- Classify errors and identify opportunities for improvement.
- Explore how healthcare AI can serve underserved populations.

## Initial Research Plan
1. Develop a small set of fictional clinical cases.
2. Evaluate AI responses using predefined criteria.
3. Check answers against reliable medical references.
4. Classify errors and document findings.
5. Publish the methodology and results transparently.

## Planned Error Categories
- Factual medical errors
- Incorrect clinical reasoning
- Missed emergency warning signs
- Potentially unsafe treatment advice
- Unsupported claims or hallucinations

## Current Status
Initial project setup. The evaluation protocol and pilot cases are yet to be developed. No research results are available yet.

## Safety
This is an educational research project, not a validated clinical tool. It must not be used to diagnose or treat patients. Only fictional cases or appropriately authorised, de-identified data will be used.

## Long-Term Vision
To help researchers improve healthcare AI and support its safe, equitable implementation across India's population.

## Research Interests
Clinical AI Evaluation | Benchmarking | Error Taxonomy | Clinical Decision Support | Health Equity | AI Deployment
